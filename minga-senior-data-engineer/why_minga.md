# Why Minga (application answer draft)

*Posting note: "Your resume and 'Why Minga' answer will be read by a real life human, so give us a glimpse of the person behind the profile." Paste the text below into the application form.*

---

My whole software career has been in education. For twelve years I've built the systems behind students at two BC universities, from the student ID card system at TRU to the pipeline that sends VIU's student data to the Ministry. All of it from the back office. Minga works on the part I've never been able to touch, which is what actually happens in a school day for kids and teachers. Getting to work on that, with the kind of data I already know how to respect, is a big part of the draw.

The other part is the role. Being the only data engineer and working out how AI-assisted data work should be reviewed is the kind of job I do my best work in. I treat this work like a hobby (it's why I started my own consulting practice), and I keep it sustainable by staying active and actually resting.

And it's here. I live in Kelowna, and three days a week in an office with people who care about what they're building sounds good to me.
