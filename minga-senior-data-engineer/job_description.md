## The Role

We’re not just hiring for a position, we’re adding a teammate

As a Senior Data Engineer at Minga, you’ll own the data platform that turns our product and business data into something 2,000+ schools, and every team at Minga, can trust. Reporting to the Senior Engineering Manager of Platform & SRE, you’ll give our Snowflake, Stitch and Metabase stack a real owner: modelling standards, orchestration, data quality, cost and access control. Over the next year you’ll also become the data lead for the ground-up rebuild of our platform.

This is a senior individual contributor role with big impact. You’ll own our data platform, influence how we grow, and help build the high-performance teams that make Minga a place people love to work.

## What You'll Do

- Own ingestion end to end: harden or replace our current Stitch pipelines and bring proper orchestration to a stack that runs on good intentions today
- Introduce and own a modelling layer (dbt or equivalent) with versioning, tests and review that our RevOps analysts can build against without slowing down
- Define and enforce access, retention and PII-handling standards across Snowflake and Metabase. We hold student-level data and we treat it that way
- Run a visible intake queue and service commitment for RevOps, Finance, CX and Product, and teach analysts to self-serve rather than wait on you
- Partner with our product squads on the analytics event model for the new platform and the tooling that maps legacy data into it as customers migrate
- Keep Snowflake and pipeline costs proportional to usage, and know exactly why when they aren’t
- Use AI tooling every day to move faster, and set the standard for how data work gets reviewed when a model can write the first draft

## What We Require

- 5+ years of experience building and operating production data pipelines
- Proven success owning a cloud data warehouse end to end (Snowflake preferred), plus dbt or a comparable modelling framework
- Experience working in a SaaS or high-growth environment, ideally as the first or only data engineer
- Strong SQL and Python skills, with working knowledge of at least one orchestration tool
- Hands-on experience with sensitive or regulated data and the access-control and privacy work that comes with it
- Strong written communication: docs, runbooks, and the ability to say no kindly
- Nice to have: exposure to student data regulations (FERPA, PIPEDA), HubSpot or Salesforce data, Metabase administration, or a warehouse migration between schemas or platforms
- There is a STRONG preference for a candidate to be in the Okanagan and be in office 3x a week. However, we are open to a remote candidate.

- Someone who brings both accountability and empathy to their work, especially when the data belongs to students and schools
